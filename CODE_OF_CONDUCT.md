# Community conduct

Treat classmates and contributors with respect. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, stalking, doxxing, and sharing another person's private information are prohibited. Share only resources you have permission to distribute. Do not impersonate university staff or imply official endorsement.

Moderators should use the minimum private information necessary to investigate a report, document actions, and allow an appeal. Project maintainers may restrict participation for violations. A private reporting channel and appeal contact must be established before public launch; community operation is not yet open.
